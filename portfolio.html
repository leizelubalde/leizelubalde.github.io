01 / EDUCATIONAL NEWSLETTER

Educational Newsletter with a Clear Next Step

Problem: A recurring newsletter gets attention but gives readers little direction on what to do next.

System: Use one topic, one practical takeaway, and one action per send. Match content to audience segments and end each issue with a next step relevant to the reader.

Outcome: More readers moving from reading to a conversation.

03 / REACTIVATION

Dormant Contact Reactivation

Problem: Inactive contacts need a relevant reason to re-engage, and regular sends need clear audience eligibility.

System: Segment inactive contacts, send three timed emails with a useful reason to respond, and use engagement across the sequence to decide who returns to regular sends.

Outcome: Bring engaged contacts back into regular sends and stop mailing contacts who don't respond.

Skills demonstrated: Engagement segmentation · Nurture planning · Suppression rules · Campaign QA

04 / NURTURE & REGISTRATION SUPPRESSION

Event Nurture & Registration Suppression

Problem: Prospects need timely reminders, while confirmed registrants should stop receiving registration invitations.

System: Build the nurture sequence and timing rules, use confirmed form submissions as the registration source, and apply registration tags before later sends. When systems are separate, export and import registrants before releasing the next email.

Outcome: More registrations, with no invitations sent to people already registered.

Skills demonstrated: Nurture workflow setup · Tagging · Send eligibility · Registration reconciliation · Campaign QA

05 / SEGMENTATION & CAMPAIGN TESTING

Engagement-Based Newsletter Program

Problem: A recurring email program needs clear audience eligibility and a way to evaluate results before expanding sends.

System: Manage engaged audience segments, exclude unsubscribed and bounced contacts, and run campaigns against prepared send and holdout groups. Review engagement, complaints, and unsubscribes to inform the next send.

Outcome: Sends go only to engaged contacts, and audience expansion is backed by test results.

Skills demonstrated: Audience segmentation · Suppression rules · Recurring email production · Send and holdout testing · Performance reporting
